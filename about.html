Our story

Open Pages began in Surabaya with a simple observation: children wanted to learn, but the books and supplies weren't there.

So two high school students started collecting and delivering them — one school and one orphanage at a time.

Why students

We're close in age to the children we serve, so the visits feel like peers, not charity.

Our vision

Equitable education across Indonesia, where every child has the materials to keep going.

How we work

We partner with local schools and communities, then report exactly where every item went.

Four programs, one goal.

Everything we run fits into these four areas.

Educational Resources & Literacy Donation

Collecting and delivering books and school supplies to orphanages and under-resourced schools.

Educational Activities & Workshops

Hosting interactive literacy, creativity and career-exploration sessions for kids.

Community Outreach & Collaboration

Partnering with local schools, brands and youth volunteers aged 15–22.

Food & Nutrition Support

Providing essential meals and snacks alongside learning initiatives.

Funded transparently, spent locally.
